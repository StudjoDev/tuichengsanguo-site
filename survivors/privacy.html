隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日　適用：Android 與 iOS 版《推城三國：倖存》（含內測版）

繁體中文English

重點摘要
  • 本遊戲是單機遊戲，不需要註冊或登入；我們沒有伺服器，也沒有玩家帳號。
  • 遊戲進度與設定只存在您的手機上。
  • 只有您自己按下按鈕時才會播放獎勵廣告，廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，而且只請求非個人化廣告。
  • 本版本沒有使用任何數據分析或當機回報服務（例如 Firebase），不會把您的遊玩紀錄傳出手機。

一、適用範圍

本政策說明手機遊戲《推城三國：倖存》（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理資料。本遊戲沒有內購，也沒有聊天、社群或使用者上傳內容的功能。

二、存在您手機上的資料（我們收不到）

  • 遊戲進度與設定：關卡紀錄與星等、軍功、校場強化、已解鎖的武將，以及音樂、音效、畫面震動等設定，只存在您的手機上，不會上傳。
  • 本機遊玩紀錄：為了排查問題，本遊戲會在手機上保留一份有大小上限（約 0.5 MB，舊的會自動刪除）的遊玩事件紀錄，例如每一局的關卡、武將、勝負、時間、升級選擇與廣告是否看完。紀錄裡沒有姓名、帳號、裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼或位置。這份紀錄不會被傳送到任何地方，本遊戲也沒有把它寄出或上傳的功能。
  • 解除安裝本遊戲，上述資料會一併刪除。我們沒有雲端存檔，因此也無法替您復原或轉移進度。

三、廣告（Google AdMob）

  • 本遊戲只有獎勵廣告：只有在您自己點選「看廣告復活」或「看廣告，軍功加倍」時才會播放。不看廣告也能完整遊玩與通關。本遊戲沒有橫幅、插頁式或開啟應用程式時自動播放的廣告。
  • 本遊戲設定為只請求非個人化廣告，不依您的興趣投放；在 iOS 上不要求「追蹤」授權（App Tracking Transparency），因此本遊戲與 AdMob 都無法取得 iOS 的廣告識別碼（IDFA）。
  • 即使是非個人化廣告，Google AdMob SDK 仍會為了投放廣告、限制播放頻率、防止詐騙與濫用、產生統計報表而處理下列資料：
    資料用途
    IP 位址（用來推估大略位置）投放廣告、防詐騙、統計
    裝置與應用程式識別碼（例如 Android 廣告 ID、App Set ID、iOS 的 IDFV）投放廣告、頻率控制、防詐騙
    裝置型號、系統版本、應用程式互動與廣告的曝光／點擊投放廣告、成效統計
    當機、效能與其他診斷資料（AdMob SDK 自身的）維持廣告正常運作
    Android 版另外可能使用 Android 的隱私沙箱 API（例如 Topics、Attribution）；iOS 版使用 Apple 的 SKAdNetwork 做不識別個人的安裝歸因。這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理：Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊、Google 隱私權政策。
  • 準備廣告前，本遊戲會透過 Google 的同意管理工具（UMP）確認您所在地區是否需要徵求同意；需要時會先顯示同意畫面，僅在工具確認可請求廣告時才載入。
  • 您可以在 Android 的「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。

四、遊玩數據分析與當機回報

本版本沒有使用任何數據分析或當機回報服務，也不會把遊玩紀錄傳出您的手機。遊戲「設定」裡的「分享匿名遊玩數據」開關，目前不會讓任何資料離開手機（因為本版本沒有可傳送的對象）；它是為日後可能加入的分析功能預留的。若日後加入這類服務，我們會先更新本政策並修改生效日期，屆時您可以用這個開關關閉傳送。

五、我們不做的事

  • 遊戲本身不收集姓名、電話、電子郵件、通訊錄、相片、麥克風、相機或精確位置。
  • 不販售任何資料，也不提供給資料經紀商。
  • 沒有內購，不處理任何付款資料。

六、兒童

本遊戲以 13 歲以上的玩家為對象，不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。如果您認為兒童透過本遊戲提供了資料給我們，請與我們聯絡。

七、您的權利與資料刪除

本遊戲沒有帳號，我們手上沒有任何可以對應到特定玩家的資料，因此無法逐一查詢或刪除；Google AdMob 處理的資料也無法由我們對應到您。您可以：在手機設定重設廣告 ID，或解除安裝本遊戲刪除手機上的所有資料。依中華民國個人資料保護法，您有查詢、更正、刪除等權利；若有疑問請來信，我們會盡力協助。

八、資料傳輸與安全

Google AdMob 的資料傳輸經過加密（HTTPS），資料可能被傳送並儲存在台灣以外地區（例如 Google 位於美國的伺服器）。

九、政策變更

政策若有變更，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日期；重大變更會在遊戲的更新說明中告知。

十、聯絡我們

來信請寄 support@tuichengsanguo.com，或見客服頁。主動來信時，我們會使用您的電子郵件地址及來信內容處理該次請求。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Applies to Android and iOS versions of 推城三國：倖存 (including beta builds)

Developer: 嬌滴滴遊戲 · Contact: support@tuichengsanguo.com

Summary
  • The game is single-player and needs no registration or sign-in. We run no servers and have no user accounts.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap a button. They are served by Google AdMob, and only non-personalized ads are requested.
  • This version does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service (such as Firebase) and does not send your gameplay data off your device.

1. Scope

This policy explains how the mobile game 推城三國：倖存 ("the Game") handles data. The Game has no in-app purchases and no chat, social or user-generated-content features.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Progress and settings (stage records and stars, merit, permanent upgrades, unlocked heroes, and music, sound-effect and screen-shake settings) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Local gameplay log: for troubleshooting, the Game keeps a size-limited log (about 0.5 MB; older entries are deleted automatically) of gameplay events such as stage, hero, win or loss, duration, level-up choices and whether an ad was completed. It contains no name, account, device identifier, advertising identifier or location. The log is never sent anywhere, and the Game has no feature to send or upload it.
  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all of the above. There is no cloud save, so we cannot restore or transfer your progress.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The Game offers rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when you tap "watch an ad to revive" or "watch an ad to double merit". You can play and finish the Game without watching any ads. There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads.
  • The Game requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, so neither the Game nor AdMob can access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Even for non-personalized ads, the Google AdMob SDK processes the following to serve ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud and abuse, and produce reports: IP address (used to estimate approximate location); device and app identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, App Set ID and the iOS IDFV); device model, OS version, app interactions and ad impressions/clicks; and the SDK's own crash, performance and other diagnostics. On Android it may also use Privacy Sandbox APIs (such as Topics and Attribution); on iOS it uses Apple's SKAdNetwork for privacy-preserving install attribution. Google processes this data under its policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Before loading ads, the Game checks through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether consent is required where you are; if so, a consent screen is shown and ads load only when UMP indicates that an ad request is permitted.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

This version does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service and does not send gameplay data off your device. The "Share anonymous gameplay data" switch in the Game's settings currently does not cause any data to leave your device (there is nothing in this version to send it to); it is reserved for analytics that may be added later. If such a service is added, we will update this policy and its effective date first, and you will be able to turn sending off with that switch.

5. What we do not do

  • The Game itself does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not sell data or share it with data brokers.
  • There are no in-app purchases and we handle no payment data.

6. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data to us through the Game.

7. Your rights and deletion

Because the Game has no accounts, we hold no data that can be linked to an individual player, so we cannot look up or delete data per player, and we cannot link data processed by Google AdMob to you. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the Game to delete all local data. If you have questions about your rights under applicable law (including Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), contact us.

8. Transfers and security

Data sent to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and may be stored outside Taiwan (for example on Google servers in the United States).

9. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes, and announce significant changes in the Game's release notes.

10. Contact

Email support@tuichengsanguo.com or see the support page. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond to your request.
