Support

If you run into a problem in Three Kingdoms: Hold the Pass, want to report a bug or have a suggestion, write to us.

support@tuichengsanguo.com

Please include

  • The game title and version number (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen)
  • Your device model and OS version
  • The screen or level where it happened and what you were doing; a screenshot helps

Purchases and restoring them

After changing phones or reinstalling, use Restore Purchases in the game's Settings. Refunds are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their policies; please request them from the store you bought from.
