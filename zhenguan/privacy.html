隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日・適用於 Android 與 iOS 版《推城三國：鎮關》（英文名：Three Kingdoms: Hold the Pass）

《推城三國：鎮關》（以下稱「本遊戲」）是一款手機塔防遊戲。本遊戲不需要註冊帳號，我們不會要求你提供姓名、電話、電子郵件等個人資料。自 1.5.0 版起，遊戲會自動建立一個匿名的線上帳號（見第二節）。本頁說明本遊戲與其使用的第三方服務會處理哪些資料。

本遊戲目前處於測試階段。功能若有調整而影響資料處理方式，我們會在新版本推出前先更新本頁與生效日期。本頁最近的更新：第二節的線上帳號（自 1.5.0 版起）；第四節的遊玩統計與當機回報（自 1.4.0 版起）。

一、首次啟動時的詢問

  • 年齡：首次啟動會請你選擇出生年份，用來判斷年齡分組（未滿 13 歲、13 至 15 歲、16 歲以上）。本遊戲只在你的裝置上保存年齡分組，不保存出生年份。
  • 同意選項：在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，16 歲以上的玩家會看到同意視窗，可以選擇全部同意、全部拒絕或自訂。拒絕也能完整遊玩。在其他地區，13 歲以上的玩家預設開啟個人化廣告與「遊玩統計與當機回報」，兩者都可以隨時在設定中關閉。
  • iOS 追蹤授權：在 iOS 上，若你可能收到個人化廣告，本遊戲會先說明用途，再由 iOS 系統詢問是否允許追蹤。未允許時只顯示非個人化廣告。

你可以隨時到遊戲內「設定 → 隱私選項」查看目前狀態、變更選擇，或重新開啟同意視窗。這些選擇只保存在你的裝置上。

二、遊戲進度與雲端存檔

遊戲進度與設定保存在你的裝置上。如果你的裝置已登入 Google Play 遊戲（Android）或 iCloud（iOS），進度會同步到你自己的帳號，方便換裝置時延續進度；這些資料由 Google 或 Apple 依其政策保存，我們讀取不到你的存檔。成就與無盡模式排行榜分數，會在你登入 Google Play 遊戲或 Game Center 時送交該平台。

「設定 → 重置所有進度」可以清除遊戲進度；已購買的項目與隱私選擇不會被清除。解除安裝本遊戲會刪除裝置上的資料。

線上帳號：自 1.5.0 版起，遊戲第一次連線時會自動替這次安裝建立一個匿名的線上帳號，用來保存之後購買的遊戲內貨幣與項目、在換手機時把它們帶過去、提供客服查詢，以及讓每日重置使用伺服器時間。這個帳號是遊戲運作所需，所有玩家都會有，不受「遊玩統計與當機回報」的開關影響。

這個帳號不需要你提供姓名、電子郵件或電話。我們的伺服器（設於台灣的 Google Cloud）會保存：匿名的登入識別碼、一組帳號代碼（顯示在「設定 → 帳號與換機」，聯絡客服時使用）、帳號建立與最後連線的時間、遊戲版本與平台、遊戲內貨幣的餘額與每一筆增減紀錄。匿名登入由 Google 的 Firebase Authentication 提供。

你可以在「設定 → 帳號與換機」產生引繼碼，把線上帳號搬到另一支手機；也可以在同一個畫面刪除線上帳號，刪除後伺服器上的上述資料會全部移除，無法復原。關卡進度不存在我們的伺服器上。

三、廣告

本遊戲含有廣告，由 Google AdMob 提供，包含你主動選擇觀看的獎勵廣告，以及戰鬥結束後偶爾出現的插頁廣告。

  • 在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，只有你在同意視窗中同意後，才會請求個人化廣告。
  • 在其他地區，13 歲以上的玩家預設會請求個人化廣告；你可以到遊戲內「設定 → 隱私選項」關閉，關閉後只請求非個人化廣告。
  • 在 iOS 上，不論地區，你沒有允許追蹤時一律只請求非個人化廣告。
  • 未滿 13 歲的玩家只會看到非個人化廣告，並向廣告服務標記為針對兒童的內容。在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，未滿 16 歲的玩家同樣只會看到非個人化廣告。

為了顯示廣告、衡量成效及防止詐騙，Google AdMob SDK 可能收集以下資料：

資料用途
IP 位址（用來推估大略位置）投放廣告、防詐騙
裝置與廣告識別碼（例如 Android 廣告 ID、iOS IDFA 或 IDFV）投放廣告、頻率控制、防詐騙；經你同意時用於個人化廣告
產品互動與廣告互動（例如應用程式啟動、廣告觀看與點擊）投放廣告、成效分析

這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊 及 Google 隱私權政策。在 Android 上，你可以到系統「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID；在 iOS 上，可以到「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」管理。

四、遊玩統計與當機紀錄

自 1.4.0 版起，本遊戲使用 Google 提供的兩項服務來了解遊戲哪裡太難、哪裡出錯：Google Analytics for Firebase（遊玩統計）與 Firebase Crashlytics（當機回報）。1.3.7 以前的版本不會傳送這些資料。

什麼時候會收集：只有在「遊玩統計與當機回報」為開啟時。歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的玩家要先在同意視窗選擇同意；其他地區 13 歲以上預設開啟。未滿 13 歲的玩家（以及歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士未滿 16 歲的玩家）一律不收集。你可以隨時到「設定 → 隱私選項」關閉，關閉後遊戲會立即停止收集與傳送。

收集的項目用途
遊戲內的操作紀錄：開啟遊戲、關卡開始與結束（關卡、模式、武將、結果、剩餘城防、波次、花費時間）、連續失敗的關卡、新手教學做到第幾步、廣告的請求與觀看結果、購買與恢復購買的結果、每日挑戰參與調整難度、改善遊戲
程式錯誤的種類、發生的程式檔名與行號；遊戲當機時的技術紀錄找出並修正錯誤
由 Google 的服務自動附上的資訊：每次安裝各自的匿名識別碼、裝置型號、作業系統版本、遊戲版本、語言、由 IP 位址推估的國家或地區計算有多少玩家繼續遊玩、把同一次安裝的紀錄歸在一起

這些資料不包含你的姓名、電子郵件、電話或精確位置，我們也沒有把它們用於廣告：在分析服務中，與廣告相關的資料使用一律設為拒絕。Google 如何處理這些資料，請見 Firebase 的隱私權與安全性說明。

五、購買

遊戲內購買由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理付款。我們看不到你的信用卡或付款資料；本遊戲只會從商店得知某個項目是否已購買，用來開通與恢復你購買的內容。

六、權限

  • 網路：用於廣告、購買與雲端存檔。沒有網路時仍可遊玩。
  • 震動（Android）：提供觸覺回饋，可在設定中關閉。
  • 廣告 ID（Android）：由廣告 SDK 使用，如上所述。

本遊戲不讀取你的通訊錄、相片、麥克風、相機或精確位置。本遊戲沒有聊天功能，也沒有玩家上傳內容的功能。

七、兒童

本遊戲不是專為兒童設計。對於表示未滿 13 歲的玩家，本遊戲只顯示非個人化廣告，不詢問 iOS 追蹤授權，也不收集遊玩統計與當機回報。如果你認為兒童透過本遊戲提供了個人資料給我們，請與我們聯絡，我們會協助處理。

八、資料的保存與刪除

我們不保存你的姓名或聯絡方式。線上帳號的資料會保存到你刪除帳號為止：你可以隨時在「設定 → 帳號與換機」刪除，或寫信請我們代為刪除（請附上帳號代碼）。遊玩統計最長保存 14 個月，當機回報保存 90 天，期滿由 Google 的服務自動刪除；若你希望提早刪除，請寫信給我們，我們可能需要請你提供協助辨識的資訊（例如遊玩的日期與時間）。保存在你裝置上的資料，解除安裝即刪除；同步到 Google Play 遊戲或 iCloud 的存檔，可以在該平台的設定中刪除；由 Google AdMob 處理的資料，請依上方連結向 Google 行使你的權利。若需要協助，請寫信給我們。

九、政策更新與聯絡我們

本政策內容若有變更，會更新本頁面並修改上方的生效日期。若有任何問題，請寫信到 support@tuichengsanguo.com，並註明遊戲名稱「推城三國：鎮關」。

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Android and iOS versions of Three Kingdoms: Hold the Pass (Chinese title: 推城三國：鎮關)

Three Kingdoms: Hold the Pass ("the game") is a mobile tower-defense game. The game does not require you to register an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, email address or similar personal details. From version 1.5.0 the game automatically creates an anonymous online account (see section 2). This page explains what data the game and the third-party services it uses may process.

The game is currently in testing. If a feature change affects how data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. Latest updates to this page: the online account in section 2 (from version 1.5.0); gameplay statistics and crash reports in section 4 (from version 1.4.0).

1. Questions at first launch

  • Age. On first launch the game asks for your year of birth to determine an age group (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 and over). Only the age group is stored, on your device. The year of birth is not stored.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, players aged 16 or over see a consent screen where they can accept all, reject all, or choose individual options. You can play the full game if you reject. In other regions, personalized ads and "gameplay statistics and crash reports" are on by default for players aged 13 or over, and either can be turned off in Settings at any time.
  • iOS tracking permission. On iOS, if you may receive personalized ads, the game first explains why and then iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you do not allow it, only non-personalized ads are shown.

You can review or change these choices at any time in Settings → Privacy Options in the game, or reopen the consent screen there. These choices are stored only on your device.

2. Game progress and cloud saves

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If your device is signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or iCloud (iOS), your progress is synced to your own account so you can continue on another device. That data is held by Google or Apple under their policies; we cannot read your save. Achievements and endless-mode leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games or Game Center when you are signed in to those services.

Settings → Reset All Progress clears your progress. Purchases and privacy choices are kept. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

Online account. From version 1.5.0, the first time the game connects it automatically creates an anonymous online account for this installation. It is used to hold in-game currency and items you buy later, to carry them to a new device, to let support look up your account, and to use server time for daily resets. The account is needed for the game to work, so every player has one, and it is not affected by the "gameplay statistics and crash reports" setting.

The account does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Our server (on Google Cloud, located in Taiwan) stores: an anonymous sign-in identifier, an account ID (shown under Settings → Account & Device Transfer, used when you contact support), when the account was created and last connected, the game version and platform, and your in-game currency balance with a record of every change. Anonymous sign-in is provided by Google's Firebase Authentication.

Under Settings → Account & Device Transfer you can create a transfer code to move the online account to another device, or delete the online account. Deleting it removes all of the data above from our server and cannot be undone. Level progress is not stored on our server.

3. Advertising

The game contains ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional interstitial ads after a battle ends.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalized ads are requested only after you agree on the consent screen.
  • In other regions, personalized ads are requested by default for players aged 13 or over. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy Options in the game, after which only non-personalized ads are requested.
  • On iOS, in every region, only non-personalized ads are requested unless you have allowed tracking.
  • Players who indicate they are under 13 see only non-personalized ads, and ad requests are marked as child-directed. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, players under 16 also see only non-personalized ads.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS you can manage tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Gameplay statistics and crash logs

From version 1.4.0, the game uses two Google services to learn where the game is too hard and where it breaks: Google Analytics for Firebase (gameplay statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). Versions up to 1.3.7 do not send this data.

When it is collected. Only while "gameplay statistics and crash reports" is on. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland must first agree on the consent screen; in other regions it is on by default for players aged 13 or over. Nothing is collected from players under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy Options, and the game stops collecting and sending immediately.

This data does not include your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not use it for advertising: every advertising-related use is set to denied in the analytics service. For how Google handles this data, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game only learns from the store whether an item has been purchased, so that it can unlock and restore what you bought.

6. Permissions

  • Network access: for ads, purchases and cloud saves. The game can be played offline.
  • Vibration (Android): haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by the ad SDK as described above.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. It has no chat and no user-generated content.

7. Children

The game is not designed specifically for children. For players who indicate they are under 13, the game shows only non-personalized ads, does not ask for iOS tracking permission, and does not collect gameplay statistics or crash reports. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us through the game, please contact us and we will help.

8. Retention and deletion

We do not hold your name or contact details. Online account data is kept until you delete the account: you can delete it at any time under Settings → Account & Device Transfer, or write to us and we will delete it for you (please include your account ID). Gameplay statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, after which Google's services delete them automatically. If you would like them deleted sooner, write to us; we may need to ask for details that help identify the records (such as the dates and times you played). Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Saves synced to Google Play Games or iCloud can be deleted in that platform's settings. For data processed by Google AdMob, please use the links above to exercise your rights with Google. If you need help, write to us.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Questions can be sent to support@tuichengsanguo.com. Please mention the game title "Three Kingdoms: Hold the Pass".
